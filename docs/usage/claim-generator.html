Claim generator

The claim generator is experimental and does not cover every claim option. Its form and the manifests it emits may change or be removed in any release. Review the output before applying it. The Requesting queues and KubeVirt integration pages remain the canonical documentation of the manifest shapes.

This page builds the documented manifests interactively, against the queues your cluster actually publishes. Run kubectl get resourceslices -o json against your cluster and paste the output below. Without a cluster, load the bundled sample inventory instead. The live match preview shows what the scheduler can choose from, not what it will pick.

Everything runs in your browser. This page makes no network request. The pasted inventory and the SSH public key never leave this page.
